Decision Engine

How to use this demo

Most agents do whatever they’re told. This engine decides first whether an AI action may happen at all, and answers execute, ask, defer, escalate or refuse.

Try it: pick a scenario, then change one thing (the amount, the evidence, how old it is) and watch the decision move.
Batch: 300 synthetic cases through the same engine.
Failure test: two cases built to break it.
About: architecture, thesis, notes and limits.
